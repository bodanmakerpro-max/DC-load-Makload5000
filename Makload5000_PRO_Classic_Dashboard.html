Current calibration · 3 points

Enter the current shown by a trusted external meter and capture the Arduino reading at each point. These 3 points are saved by the Nano firmware and affect its current measurement and feedback.

Voltage calibration

Apply a known DC voltage and enter the voltage shown by your multimeter. The dashboard calculates a correction factor.

Factor: 1.0000 (uncalibrated)
Current calibration is saved in the Arduino EEPROM through CAL commands and affects current feedback. Voltage calibration is a browser-only display correction and does not change the Arduino overvoltage trip.
